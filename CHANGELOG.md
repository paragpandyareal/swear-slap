# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-10)

First public release.

- Swear at Claude and a cartoon hand slaps the band above your prompt. The message is not sent, and a polite version goes back in your prompt box.
- Animation: wind-up, swing with speed lines, an impact flash, a SLAP! burst with comic dots, cracked glass, dizzy stars, a coin into the swear jar, and a fade-out. It fits whatever room the band has, with the whole hand always in view.
- Pixels drawn with quadrant blocks (2 by 2 per cell) in colours that 256-colour terminals show exactly.
- Sound plays by itself on Mac, Windows and Linux. Over SSH it rings your terminal bell. `/slap sound test` says what it detected and what happened.
- Commands: `/slap on`, `/slap off`, `/slap jar`, `/slap reset`, `/slap sound auto|bell|off|test`.
- Start a message with `~ ` to skip the check once. Code blocks and pasted text are never checked.
