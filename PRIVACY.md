# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Swear Slap collects nothing.

- **No data leaves your computer.** The mod makes no network requests, has no analytics and no telemetry, and talks to no server, including ours (there isn't one).
- **What it reads:** the message you are about to send, only to check it for swear words, on your own machine. A message with a swear word is stopped before it reaches Claude.
- **What it keeps:** four small values in Claude Code's own storage on your computer: the swear jar total, the number of slaps, whether the slap is on, and your sound choice. `/slap reset` empties the jar.
- **What it never does:** read your files, read your conversation, or send anything anywhere.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/paragpandyareal/swear-slap/issues.
